Format

One setting per line as @key: value. Lines starting with # are comments.

@title: Present Simple
@instructions: Choose the correct answer.

# multiple_choice: radio buttons
@type: multiple_choice
@question: He _____ his homework.
@options: do, does, doing
@correct: does

# dropdown: a select box
@type: dropdown
@question: She _____ a student.
@options: is, are, am
@correct: is

# fill_blank: free text, checked case-insensitively
@type: fill_blank
@question: They _____ to school by bus.
@correct: go

@correct closes a question. Options are separated by commas.
